  1. 1

    shadcn-svelte

    shadcn-svelte.com ↗

    A catalogue you copy into your own repo, on the bits-ui behaviour layer. You own the markup and the design tokens.

    Updates, 6 months
    12 bits-ui releases
    Users
    3.85M downloads / month
    Stars
    9 131 shadcn-svelte repo

    For

    • By far the largest Svelte install base — and bits-ui targets Svelte only, so that figure is all Svelte usage
    • You own the component code: if the project stopped, your app keeps working
    • Richest catalogue of finished pieces: Data Table, Chart, Sidebar, Sonner
    • Steady cadence, a release roughly every two weeks

    Against

    • bits-ui and shadcn-svelte share one lead maintainer (63% and 55% of commits): one person, two layers
    • No tour component — this demo borrows Ark's
    • Most files to maintain after Ark: 11 component files here against 2 for Skeleton
    • Copied code means no automatic upstream fixes

    Longevity for Svelte: the safest of the three. bits-ui exists only for Svelte, so its 3.85M monthly downloads are not borrowed from a React ecosystem. And the copy-paste model is itself insurance: an abandonment costs you upstream fixes, not a working application.

  2. 2

    Ark UI

    ark-ui.com ↗

    The widest headless catalogue, on the Zag.js state machines. Behaviour only — every pixel of styling is yours.

    Updates, 6 months
    11 + 15 for Zag engine
    Users
    43 675 Svelte downloads / month
    Stars
    5 394 ark repo

    For

    • 67 components, including Tour and Steps, which neither other library has
    • The most actively developed: its Zag engine shipped 15 releases in 6 months
    • Commercially backed by the Chakra ecosystem, whose React line pulls 6.6M downloads a month
    • You own the styling, same as shadcn

    Against

    • Its Svelte binding is ~1% of Ark's usage: 43 675 a month against 3.86M for React
    • Ships no styling at all — every component is yours to dress
    • Its Tour needed two undocumented CSS fixes to work at all in this build
    • The most verbose: 495 lines of component code here against 81 for Skeleton

    Longevity for Svelte: the engine is the safest bet of all — Zag.js pulls 5.09M downloads a month and powers Chakra, Ark and Skeleton alike, so it outlives any one wrapper. The risk is specific to Svelte: the binding is a minority citizen in its own ecosystem and could be deprioritised against React.

  3. 3

    Skeleton

    skeleton.dev ↗

    A complete design system installed as a dependency: themes, utility classes and components, decided for you.

    Updates, 6 months
    6 releases
    Users
    155 680 downloads / month
    Stars
    6 061 skeleton repo

    For

    • Fewest files to maintain: 2 component files here against 11 and 12
    • 24 themes provided; switching one is a single word
    • Smallest JavaScript bundle: 192 kB against 339 and 382
    • Runs on Zag.js, so the engine underneath is as safe as Ark's

    Against

    • Smallest install base: 155 680 a month, 25× below bits-ui
    • Slowest cadence of the three: 6 releases in 6 months
    • Its interactive components ship no styling — the CSS layer covers forms, buttons, cards and tables only
    • No theme is black and white; the least colourful, hamlindigo, still sits at chroma 0.052

    Longevity for Svelte: the weakest position, for one structural reason. It is the only one of the three that owns your visual layer — with shadcn and Ark the CSS is in your repo, so an abandonment is survivable. Here you would inherit a design system you did not write. Its Zag engine would survive; its styling would not.
